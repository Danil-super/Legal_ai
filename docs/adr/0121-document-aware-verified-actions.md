# ADR 0121 — Include document context in independently reviewed actions

Date: 2026-11-05. Status: accepted; authorized audit remediation.

The audit found that clinic documents were available to the research model only
for free recommendations/drafts that Core deliberately does not publish. The
independent reviewer received neither clinic documents nor case attachments.
Consequently, the reviewer could not check the researcher's use of those texts.

Both reasoning passes now receive the same existing bounded, de-identified clinic
and case document excerpts. No new document category, provider, private database
identifier, raw file, or permission is introduced. Readiness checklists remain
non-authoritative and are not sent to the reviewer.

Approved clinic templates may guide conditional checks and ACTION alternatives,
whose legal basis must still come from approved normative evidence. A template
does not establish the patient's signature, applicable contract edition, warranty,
medical facts or liability. Actions must retain unknown applicability conditions
and request comparison with the signed document when needed. Mandatory normative
evidence prevails over internal wording; conflict hints are warnings, not findings.
Case attachment text remains unconfirmed context and cannot overwrite facts.

Citation allowlists, semantic review and deterministic source/date/fact/risk gates
are unchanged. Free internalRecommendations and patientDraft are still excluded
from the published verified plan. No clinic/case fragment becomes a legal citation.

Tests reproduce the previously absent reviewer channels, check that context does
not alter facts/evidence, and pass conditional document-aware ACTIONs through the
real Core verifier/publication filter. Synthetic model responses prove plumbing
and fail-closed behavior only; legal adequacy needs a live model/expert review.
